Module 4 · X-ray vision

In the code: a miss is a no, a no is forever, and a yes barely helps.

The score, as the code keeps it

Illustration of the rule, not a real score log (Rook doesn't keep score history).

  • offer.py: anything that isn't a yes, including running out of time, takes points off.
  • history.py: −0.12 for a no or a miss, +0.08 for a yes. A 2019 note asks whether scores should recover. Nobody decided.
  • From the bottom it takes about 10–12 yeses in a row, at about one ping a week: roughly 3 months with no misses.

Hypothesis 1 (most likely)

Based on what I found, the reason some responders are getting no pings at all is that a few missed pings in the first days after the release pushed their score to the bottom, and the system gives them no way back up, because the shorter 60-second clock turned slow answers into misses, each miss is penalised like a no and never wears off, and the system stops at the first yes.

In one breath

4.2 gave people less time to answer and kept an old rule that treats a missed ping like a no forever. Four willing responders were pushed out within a week.

The fix makes a miss cost less, lets a bad week fade, shows handlers why, and tells everyone first. The checklist makes sure the next brief names an owner, a measure, a fixed scope and the problem before anyone ships a fix.
